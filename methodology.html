THE SCIENCE
OF PRESSURE.

SupeRR Selector evaluates performance before each delivery and compares pressure performance against a non-pressure baseline.

01 / SECOND INNINGS

Chase pressure

Required run rate at least 9; or final 18 legal balls with required rate at least 7; or six or more wickets down with 36 or fewer legal balls remaining.

02 / FIRST INNINGS

Innings pressure

Four or more wickets down after 70% of available legal balls; or at least 85% of available legal balls completed.

03 / BATTING SCORE

Batting impact

Pressure runs, balls faced and strike rate are compared with a non-pressure baseline for the same format, innings and phase. A sample-reliability adjustment reduces small-sample influence.

04 / BOWLING SCORE

Bowling impact

Pressure runs conceded, legal balls and bowler-credited wickets are compared with non-pressure phase baselines, with reliability adjustment.

05 / QUALIFICATION

Minimum sample

Batters require at least 30 pressure balls. Bowlers require at least 30 pressure legal balls. Super Overs are excluded from regular rankings.

06 / INTERPRETATION

Reference score: 100

100 is the baseline reference. A score above 100 indicates better performance than the relevant non-pressure benchmark after reliability adjustment. Scores are not probabilities or predictions.
